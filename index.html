Where I work

My work sits where environmental science meets software: building the data behind climate and sustainability claims so that it is measurable, verifiable and able to stand up to audit.

Turning carbon methodologies and accounting standards into digital systems that make climate claims auditable.

  • Data-provider integration for forest carbon
  • Digital MRV system and Scope 1 & 2 emissions API at CarboBon

Earth observation & AI

Building models that turn LiDAR, radar and optical imagery into decision-grade measurements of forests and landscapes.

  • AI yield forecasting from UAV-LiDAR
  • SAR–optical fusion for timber volume
  • Mangrove mapping with AlphaEarth embeddings

Environmental risk intelligence

Applying spatiotemporal analytics to understand how environmental risks shift across places and over time.

  • Re-evaluating U.S. wildfire risk (Natural Hazards)
  • High-resolution urban heat modeling (CaGIS)

Verra · 2026–present

Digitalizing carbon methodologies

Challenge

Carbon methodologies live in long technical documents, which makes project design, calculation and review slow and error-prone.

What I did

  • Translate nature-based carbon methodologies into digital policies and calculation logic across Verra Project Hub and the Guardian platform (Hedera).
  • Lead testing and release, from requirements through QA, UAT and production.
  • Write API documentation and developer guides so project developers can submit data directly.

Outcome

  • Six methodology policies taken through to release on the Guardian platform, spanning agriculture, afforestation, forest management and blue carbon.
  • Calculation-logic corrections that protect the accuracy of credit outcomes.

About

About me

I build environmental data that stands up to audit. I've worked on all three sides of carbon measurement: developing the measurement models during my Ph.D., building a digital MRV system at a startup, and now turning carbon methodologies into software inside a standard-setter.

Today at Verra I work where standards meet software: turning carbon methodologies into digital systems and connecting remote-sensing data providers to the registry. I'm most interested in how AI and Earth observation can make environmental claims of every kind trustworthy. My base is nature-based carbon markets, and I'm extending that work into supply-chain (Scope 3) emissions and broader environmental intelligence.

Before Verra, I built the data foundations at Virginia Tech: a Ph.D. in geospatial and environmental analysis using LiDAR, satellite imagery and machine learning. Alongside it, I helped build a digital MRV system at a startup.

It all started in Bangladesh's development sector, where I managed sanitation, waste and urban programs with municipalities and NGOs. That work taught me that environmental decisions are only as good as the data behind them. I've since worked across Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Indonesia and the United States.
